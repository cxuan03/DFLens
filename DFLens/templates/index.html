The Problem

Why Deepfake Detection Matters

AI-generated deepfakes are spreading at unprecedented speed — and the human eye can no longer keep up.

📰
Misinformation Crisis
Deepfake videos of politicians and public figures are weaponised to spread false narratives and manipulate elections worldwide.
💸
Financial Fraud
In 2024, a Hong Kong employee was tricked into transferring $25M via a deepfake video call impersonating the company CFO.
🧠
Humans Cannot Detect Them
Studies show humans correctly identify deepfakes only 54% of the time — barely better than random chance.
📈
Exponential Growth
The number of deepfakes online doubles every 6 months. Over 500,000 deepfake videos are shared on social media every day.
🔒
Identity & Biometric Fraud
Deepfake technology is used to bypass facial recognition systems and commit identity theft at scale.
⚖️
Legal Challenges
Deepfakes are being submitted as false evidence in court. Reliable AI detection tools are now essential for justice systems.

The Technology

How DFLens Works

A four-step AI pipeline from media upload to detection verdict.

1
Upload Media
User uploads an image (JPG/PNG) or video (MP4/MOV) through the web interface.
2
Frame Extraction
For videos, 16 frames are evenly sampled across the full duration for comprehensive coverage.
3
AI Classification
CLIP ViT-B/32 analyses each frame, detecting subtle AI manipulation artefacts invisible to humans.
4
Final Verdict
Majority voting across all frames produces a REAL/FAKE verdict with a full confidence score.
